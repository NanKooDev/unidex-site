Unidex privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Unidex is a free companion app for tracking your living dex and looking up game data. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: your data stays on your phone and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives it.

What the app stores on your device

Your dexes and progress (which Pokémon you have seen or caught), shiny hunts, teams, sprite sources you added, and your settings. This stays in the app's private storage on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive in Settings, the app copies the data above to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your devices stay in sync.

  • The app asks only for the drive.appdata permission. That covers its own hidden folder; it cannot see or change any of your other files.
  • The data goes directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It is not sent to any server run by the developer.
  • Google's handling of your Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • To stop syncing, choose Stop syncing in Settings. To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps → Unidex and choose Delete hidden app data. You can remove the app's access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps.

Sprite sources you add

Unidex comes without Pokémon pictures. If you add a sprite source in Settings, the app downloads pictures from the address you entered. That server receives normal web requests from your phone, such as its IP address. Which servers are used is entirely up to you, and the developer has no control over them.

Export and import

Export creates a backup file and opens your phone's share menu; where it goes is your choice. Import reads a file you pick.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts with the developer, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or other files.

The app needs only the internet permission, used for sync and for sprite sources you added.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
